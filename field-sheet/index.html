      Scope of the calculation
      
        
      Limits
      
        
      
          
      
            This sheet applies the weighted environmental impact and the multicriteria score from
            Zure, Sung, Rahim and Kuo,
            International Journal of Molecular Sciences 2024, volume 25, article 6009.
            doi:10.3390/ijms25116009
          
      
          
      
            Six profiles are scored safe = 1, mild = 2 or danger = 3. They are atmospheric
            hydroxylation, soil adsorption, fish biotransformation half-life, bioconcentration,
            bioaccumulation and biodegradation half-life. Environmental impact is the total divided
            by 18, written as a percent.
          
      
          
      
            A total of 6 is safe. Totals from 7 to 11 are mild. Totals from 12 to 18 are danger.
            The all-safe case is 6/18, which is 33.33 percent. A cutoff at exactly 33.33 percent
            would mislabel it, so the sheet uses the integer bands.
          
      
          
      
            The overall score is wE times Enorm, plus wS times Snorm, plus wC times Cnorm. The
            default weights are equal. Enorm gives 100 to the most negative binding energy in the
            set. Snorm gives 100 to the lowest environmental impact in the set. Cnorm gives 100 to
            the lowest cost in the set.
          
      
          
      
            The paper writes the efficacy formula so the most negative binding energy would score 0.
            That fights the stated aim. This sheet gives that value a score of 100. The paper's cost
            equation uses the letter S where the cost belongs. This sheet uses the cost.
          
      
          
      
            Scores are relative to the candidates on this sheet. Add or remove a row and the
            normalized scores change. If every candidate shares a value, or there is only one
            candidate, that normalized score is 50.
          
      
          
      
            The worked example uses the illustrative rows in this repository. Those numbers are not
            a published table. Bin your own EPI Suite output before you enter a tier.
          
      
          
      
            This sheet does not close a pre-harvest antiviral mass balance. That calculation has its
            own field sheet in
            antiviral-mass-balance.
            Plant identification has its own sheet in
            field-botanist-dendrologist-agent.
          
      
          
      Records remain in this browser. There is no account. Clearing site data deletes the stored records.
